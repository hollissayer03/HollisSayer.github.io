VIEW PROJECT

Research & evaluation

Final-year dissertation

Conducted a comparative study of traditional and AI assisted design, evaluating how generative AI interpreted a shared brief into level-design proposals covering scene structure, mechanic introductions and asset use. I implemented both designs in Unreal Engine and analysed quantitative and qualitative feedback from 12 participants. Findings favoured traditional design across all measured variables, including teaching mechanics, perceived flow and layout clarity, highlighting the need for human judgement and refinement when using AI generated proposals.
