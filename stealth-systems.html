CASE STUDY / 01

The crouching system

Design challenge

Teach crouching through the world itself, without relying on text-heavy tutorial instructions.

Design decision

I chose a mouse running through the tunnel as a diegetic teaching cue. Its movement draws attention to the route, while its reaction to the player invites experimentation with quieter movement. This lets the player discover crouching through observation and action.

IMPLEMENTATION / BLUEPRINTS

Technical breakdown

AI patrol — waypoint traversal

The Patrol custom event moves the soldier toward the current entry in its Waypoints array. On success or failure, the graph advances Current Waypoint, compares it with the array length, and resets the index to zero when the route is complete before calling Patrol again.

AI sight & hearing detection

AI hearing — responding to player noise

On Hear Noise casts the instigator to the first-person character and stores a player reference. A hearing counter feeds a branch that either starts the hearing response or returns the soldier to patrol. The response checks the reference before moving toward the player and transitioning to shooting.

AI sight — checking visibility before pursuit

On See Pawn stores the character reference and performs a visibility trace between the soldier and player. The hit result feeds a branch before Soldier Sight runs. The sight response checks the reference, moves toward the player, then stops movement and calls Shooting.
