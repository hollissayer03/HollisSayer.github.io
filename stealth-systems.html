CASE STUDY / 01

Teaching the player how to play the game

Design challenge

How do I ensure that the player learns the controls and how to play without intrusive, text-heavy instructions that can harm the player’s agency, damage immersion, and break flow and pacing?

Design decision

Crouching:

I chose a mouse running through a low, raised tunnel as a diegetic teaching cue. Its movement draws attention to the route, and the tunnel’s height teaches the player to crouch to traverse it.

Stealth Attacking

After the player successfully leaves the spawn area, they are rewarded with a knife. This draws on a familiar player schema: in a stealth game, a knife suggests a weapon for close-range killing, giving the player a clear use to test without an explicit instruction.

Applying the crouching and knife mechanics to learn the AI behaviour and alert states in a controlled environment

There are two instances where this is applied. The first lets the player learn the AI alert states and test out the knife. The second lets them learn enemy sightlines and patrolling, gaining an insight into future challenges.

IMPLEMENTATION / BLUEPRINTS

Technical breakdown

AI Logic

AI patrol — waypoint traversal

The Patrol custom event moves the soldier toward the current entry in its Waypoints array. On success or failure, the graph advances Current Waypoint, compares it with the array length, and resets the index to zero when the route is complete before calling Patrol again.

AI hearing logic

On Hear Noise casts the instigator to the first-person character and stores a player reference. A hearing counter feeds a branch that either starts the hearing response or returns the soldier to patrol. The response checks the reference before moving toward the player and transitioning to shooting.

AI sight Logic

On See Pawn stores the character reference and performs a visibility trace between the soldier and player. The hit result feeds a branch before Soldier Sight runs. The sight response checks the reference, moves toward the player, then stops movement and calls Shooting.
